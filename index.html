1. Introduction, Scope & Browser Distribution Scope

This Privacy Policy describes how MR Software Studio handles information inside the JobFill BD Manifest V3 browser extension (version 2.0.0) across Microsoft Edge and Google Chrome. JobFill BD helps Bangladeshi job applicants organize application profiles, resize and validate photos and signatures locally, discover official public job circulars, track application deadlines, and fill supported recruitment web forms under explicit user review.

This public page at https://mahfuzar148.github.io/jobfill-bd-privacy/ runs no external JavaScript, sets no cookies, embeds no external fonts or analytics trackers, and contains no data-collection forms.

3. Personal Profile Information

If you choose to create local applicant profiles, you may save fields required for Bangladeshi public and private recruitment applications:

  • Identity & Family Details: Full name (English/Bengali), father's and mother's names (English/Bengali), spouse's name (if applicable), date of birth, gender, nationality, marital status, and religion (where requested by application portals).
  • Government Identifiers & Quotas: National ID (NID), Birth Registration Number, Passport number and expiry date, quota category, freedom-fighter or disability quota/certificate details, and departmental/employment status.
  • Contact & Addresses: Mobile number, confirm mobile, email address, and present/permanent addresses (care of, village/road/house/flat, division, district, upazila, post office, post code).
  • Recruitment & Physical Attributes: Physical measurements (height, weight, chest), blood group, exam center, question version, cadre/non-cadre/organization choices, and referee contact details.

4. Education & Employment Information

  • Secondary & Higher Secondary (SSC / HSC): Examination name, board, institution, roll number, registration number, group/subject, result type, GPA/scale, and passing year.
  • Higher Education & Additional Qualifications: Graduation and Masters degrees, major/subject, university/college, student ID/roll/registration/session, CGPA, course duration, appeared status, exam dates, and training/diploma/computer/language skills.
  • Employment History: Employer category, organization name, designation, work address, start/end dates, current work status, salary, and job responsibilities.

Normalized records written to education_records and experience_records are stored as AES-256-GCM ciphertexts inside browser IndexedDB.

5. Documents, Photos & Signatures

  • Supported Categories: Applicant Photograph (photo), Applicant Signature (signature), National ID (nid), Birth Certificate (birth_certificate), Educational Certificate (educational_certificate), and Other files (other).
  • Local Image Formatting: Photo resizing (e.g., 300×300 px) and signature resizing (e.g., 300×80 px) run entirely inside your browser via HTML5 Canvas, storing a processed copy while keeping your original file intact without uploading image bytes externally.
  • Local-Only Scope Across Audited v2.0.0 Releases: Plaintext dataUrl payloads are omitted before saving; payloads are encrypted with AES-256-GCM in the local documents store and attached to a form file input only after explicit user approval (GET_APPROVED_DOCUMENT_PAYLOAD). Uploaded documents, photos, signatures, and certificates remain strictly local in the audited v2.0.0 release and are excluded from Cloud Sync even in Chrome.

6. Autofill & Active Webpage Access

JobFill BD interacts with open webpages only to scan form inputs and assist with populating your application fields under your review:

  • Declared & On-Demand Scope: Bundled content scripts target declared Bangladeshi recruitment domains (*.teletalk.com.bd, *.gov.bd, *.bb.org.bd, *.org.bd, *.ac.bd, *.bdjobs.com). Custom non-declared websites are accessed only after user interaction via activeTab or explicit per-site permission through optional_host_permissions.
  • Hard Site Exclusions: Google Meet, Zoom, YouTube, Google Workspace/Accounts (Mail, Drive, Docs, Calendar, Login), and browser internal pages are excluded statically via 15 exclude_matches rules in manifest.json and dynamically in site-policy.ts. JobFill BD never hooks getUserMedia, getDisplayMedia, RTCPeerConnection, page fetch, or XMLHttpRequest.
  • Human-in-the-Loop & Unencrypted HTTP Safeguards: Form scanning runs deterministically inside your browser with zero calls to external AI services. Placing sensitive identifiers onto non-HTTPS (http://) pages prompts an explicit warning overlay first. JobFill BD never clicks "Final Submit" or payment buttons and never bypasses CAPTCHA or SMS/OTP gates.

7. Public Job Circular Source Requests

Discovering new official job circulars and deadlines requires an active Internet connection on manual refresh or scheduled background alarms:

  • Read-Only GET Without Saved Personal Data: Job fetching uses singlePublicGet() with HTTP GET and credentials: 'omit'. It sends no saved profile details, NID, phone numbers, uploaded files, cookies, or authentication tokens.
  • Configured Public Sources: Public circular adapters query Teletalk Alljobs (alljobs.teletalk.com.bd), Bangladesh National Portal & listed ministry/university portals (bangladesh.gov.bd, mopa.gov.bd, dshe.gov.bd, doict.gov.bd, dme.gov.bd, techedu.gov.bd, dghs.gov.bd, cabinet.gov.bd, mof.gov.bd, www.du.ac.bd, www.buet.ac.bd, petrobangla.org.bd), BPSC (bpsc.gov.bd), Bangladesh Bank (erecruitment.bb.org.bd), NTRCA (ntrca.gov.bd), DPE (dpe.gov.bd), Bdjobs public JSON (storage.googleapis.com), Google News RSS (news.google.com), Remotive, Remote OK, Himalayas, Arbeitnow, and optional ReliefWeb API v2 (api.reliefweb.int — contacted only if you enter your own approved ReliefWeb appname parameter in Preferences, which is passed in the query string to ReliefWeb).
  • Anti-Bot & CAPTCHA Respect: Automated extraction stops immediately and enters cooldown backoff if any portal serves a CAPTCHA or anti-bot gate.

8. Edge Store Isolation vs. Chrome Optional Supabase Cloud Sync

Microsoft Edge Add-ons Store v2.0.0: Cloud Sync Disabled & Unreachable In the Microsoft Edge Store v2.0.0 release, built with compile-time flag __JOBFILL_CLOUD_SYNC_DISABLED__=true, optional Supabase authentication, profile/activity Cloud Sync, and shared cloud job DB reads are completely disabled and compiled out. The Edge Store release cannot enable Cloud Sync, hides all cloud configuration UI, and sends zero signup, login, profile upload, or activity upload requests.

A. Chrome Optional Supabase Cloud Sync Configuration (Opt-In Only)

In Google Chrome / Cloud-Sync-enabled builds (where __JOBFILL_CLOUD_SYNC_DISABLED__ is false), optional Cloud Sync remains off by default (cloudSyncEnabled: false) and operates solely if you explicitly enter your own Supabase Project URL (HTTPS) and public Anon Key inside Security & Backup and enable it. Storing Supabase service_role keys inside extension configuration is rejected by code (isSafePublicAnonKey).

B. Exact Data Synchronized in Chrome When Opted In

When Cloud Sync is explicitly enabled in a Cloud-Sync-enabled Chrome build and you trigger synchronization using your local Sync Passphrase (uploadEncryptedVaultToCloud):

  • Synced Scopes (profiles_bundle & activity_bundle only):
    • profiles_bundle: Your saved applicant profiles (names, family/address details, education and work experience fields stored inside your profile objects, contact details, IDs, quota/referee fields), serialized as JSON and encrypted client-side before upload.
    • activity_bundle: Your job bookmarks (saved job ID and personal bookmark note) and job applications tracker records, serialized as JSON and encrypted client-side before upload.
  • Excluded From Cloud Sync: Uploaded documents, photos, signatures, and certificates (the local documents store / documents_bundle) remain strictly local-only on your device in v2.0.0 and are not uploaded.
  • Remote Tables Updated: Encrypted blobs are written via HTTPS POST to /rest/v1/user_vault_blobs (enforcing per-user isolation via PostgreSQL Row Level Security). Deletion calls target /rest/v1/user_vault_blobs and /rest/v1/user_sync_devices.

C. Client-Side AES-256-GCM Encryption & Explicit Authentication Non-E2EE Boundary

  • How Synced Vault Blobs Are Encrypted: Before leaving Chrome, profiles_bundle and activity_bundle are encrypted locally using Web Crypto AES-256-GCM (12-byte random IV) with a key derived via 100,000 iterations of PBKDF2-SHA256 (16-byte random salt) from your Sync Passphrase (minimum 6 characters). The code checks assertValidCiphertextEnvelope and refuses to upload plaintext bundles; your Sync Passphrase is never sent to Supabase, so Supabase servers cannot decrypt those ciphertext payloads.
  • Authentication Information & Metadata Are NOT End-to-End Encrypted: When you sign up, sign in, or sign out via Supabase Auth (supabase-auth.ts -> /auth/v1/signup, /auth/v1/token?grant_type=password, /auth/v1/logout), your email address and account password are sent over HTTPS TLS (transport-encrypted, not E2EE) directly to the configured Supabase Auth service so it can authenticate you and issue a session token. In addition, Supabase sees standard account/database metadata: your authenticatedEmail / user UUID, configured endpoint origin, Row Level Security user ID, record_scope (profiles_bundle or activity_bundle), schema_version, sync timestamps (client_updated_at, updated_at), device metadata if recorded, and standard HTTP connection metadata (such as IP address). Short-lived JWT access tokens stay in volatile memory while signed in and clear on sign-out.

D. Optional Chrome Shared Public Cloud Jobs Feed

In Cloud-Sync-enabled Chrome builds, if you separately enable Shared Public Cloud Jobs Feed (usePublicCloudJobsFeed), public-jobs-client.ts issues unauthenticated read-only HTTP GET requests (credentials: 'omit') to /rest/v1/jobs on your configured Supabase project without user auth tokens or personal profile data.

10. Local Encryption & Security Practices

  • Local AES-256-GCM Vault: Profile vaults, normalized education/employment rows, and uploaded documents in IndexedDB are encrypted using Web Crypto AES-256-GCM with unique 12-byte random IVs. By default, encryption relies on a non-extractable device CryptoKey inside IndexedDB.
  • Optional Master Vault Password: Enabling a Master Vault Password derives keys locally using 100,000 iterations of PBKDF2-SHA256 and a random 16-byte salt. Your plaintext password is never saved to storage; only a verification salt/hash is saved locally, while unlocked keys stay in transient memory until manual lock or inactivity timeout (default 15 minutes).
  • Browser Hygiene: Extension pages enforce Manifest V3 Content Security Policy (script-src 'self'; object-src 'self';), use zero eval() or remote script execution, mask sensitive identifiers in local history logs, and strip URL query/hash strings before recording autofill sessions.

11. User Consent, Operating Controls & Cloud Opt-In / Opt-Out

  • Local-Only Default: Both Edge v2.0.0 and Chrome operate locally out of the box without any account requirement.
  • Active-Tab Consent & HTTP Warnings: Custom third-party career sites require explicit user interaction and browser host permission grant. Unencrypted http:// recruitment pages trigger an interactive warning overlay before placing sensitive identifiers.
  • Chrome Cloud Sync Control & Opt-Out: Where Cloud Sync exists in a Chrome build, users must supply explicit Supabase configuration and opt in manually; preview-before-merge/replace (previewCloudVaultPull) prevents silent overwrites, and users can sign out, disable Cloud Sync, or delete synced vault rows (deleteAllPrivateCloudData()) anytime. In Microsoft Edge Store v2.0.0, Cloud Sync cannot be enabled at all.

12. Data Retention & Deletion

  • Local Storage Retention: Profiles, documents, bookmarks, and application tracker entries remain inside your browser profile until deleted by you or upon uninstalling the extension. Autofill history caps at 100 sessions; runtime diagnostics hold at most 50 hostname-only records in memory.
  • In-Extension Local Deletion: In Security & Backup, Delete Local Private Vault Data (deleteLocalUserData()) deletes local profiles, education/employment rows, uploaded documents, vault keys, bookmarks, applications, notifications, and autofill logs while retaining cached public job listings. Purge All Profiles & Data (purgeAllData()) removes every IndexedDB store and extension storage key completely.
  • Chrome Cloud Vault Deletion Boundary: In Cloud-enabled Chrome builds, clicking Delete Cloud Data sends authenticated HTTP DELETE calls to remove user_vault_blobs and user_sync_devices rows from your configured Supabase project. Deleting the underlying Supabase Auth user account record itself requires your Supabase backend's optional delete-account Edge Function or direct deletion in your Supabase project dashboard.
  • Uninstallation: Removing JobFill BD from Microsoft Edge or Google Chrome deletes browser-managed extension storage.

13. Local Backup & Restore

  • Manual JSON Export: Export your applicant profiles locally via exportProfileData() as either a PBKDF2 + AES-256-GCM password-encrypted backup (recommended) or unencrypted JSON.
  • Schema-Validated Restore: Restoring a backup (importProfileData()) validates schema offline in browser memory and prompts for your decryption passphrase when encrypted. Binary document uploads reside in IndexedDB rather than the JSON profile export file.

14. Notifications & Job Reminders

  • MV3 Alarms & Local Matching: Background alarms (jobfill_daily_job_check, default 9:00 AM Asia/Dhaka, and jobfill_deadline_scheduler) match cached public circulars against local preferences inside your browser and display notifications via chrome.notifications.
