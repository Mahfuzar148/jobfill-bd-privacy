Privacy Policy for JobFill BD

Product: JobFill BD (Browser Extension)
Publisher: MR Software Studio
Effective Date: November 11, 2026
Last Updated: November 11, 2026
Core Architecture Summary (Verified Against Codebase) JobFill BD operates in Local-Only mode by default (cloudSyncEnabled: false, no account required). Applicant profiles, academic history, work records, uploaded photos/signatures/certificates, bookmarks, and application tracker logs are stored locally in your browser. Optional remote cloud backup occurs only if you explicitly configure your own Supabase backend and turn on Cloud Sync, and public job circular checks perform read-only HTTP GET requests without your saved profile data.

1. Introduction, Overview & Scope

This Privacy Policy explains how MR Software Studio handles information inside the JobFill BD Manifest V3 browser extension (compatible with Google Chrome and Microsoft Edge, version 2.0.0). JobFill BD is built to help Bangladeshi job applicants organize application profiles, resize and validate photos/signatures locally, discover public job circulars, track application deadlines, and fill supported recruitment web forms under explicit user review.

This document describes only the actual behavior implemented in JobFill BD's source code. This static webpage itself executes no external JavaScript, sets no cookies, uses no analytics or tracking scripts, and contains no forms collecting personal information.

3. Personal Profile Information

When you create one or more local applicant profiles, you may voluntarily save the following categories defined in the extension's profile schema:

  • Identity & Family Details: Full name (English and Bengali), father's name (English/Bengali), mother's name (English/Bengali), spouse's name (if applicable), date of birth, gender, nationality, marital status, and religion (used only where government forms request it).
  • Government Identifiers & Quotas: National ID (NID), Birth Registration Number, Passport number and expiry date, quota category, freedom-fighter or disability certificate references, and departmental/employment status.
  • Contact & Address Information: Mobile number, confirmation mobile, email address, and present/permanent addresses (care of, village/road/house/flat, division, district, upazila, post office, post code).
  • Recruitment-Specific Metadata & Referees: Physical measurements (height, weight, chest), blood group, exam center, question version, cadre/non-cadre/organization choices, and referee names, designations, organizations, phone numbers, and emails.

4. Education & Employment Information

You may store structured academic and career records inside your local profile and normalized IndexedDB stores:

  • Secondary & Higher Secondary (SSC / HSC): Examination name, education board, institution, roll number, registration number, group/subject, result type, GPA/scale, and passing year.
  • Higher Education & Additional Qualifications: Graduation and Masters degrees, subject/major, university/institute, college, student ID/roll/registration/session, CGPA/result, course duration, appeared status, exam dates, and training/diploma/computer/language skills.
  • Job Experience: Employer category (government, semi-government, autonomous, private, NGO, etc.), organization name, designation, employer address, start/end dates, current work status, salary, and job responsibilities.

When saved via the active IndexedDB encryption path, records in education_records and experience_records are stored as encrypted ciphertexts rather than plaintext rows.

5. Documents, Photos & Signatures

You may upload files into the local document manager for job application readiness:

  • Supported Categories: Applicant Photograph (photo), Applicant Signature (signature), National ID (nid), Birth Certificate (birth_certificate), Educational Certificate (educational_certificate), and Other supporting files (other).
  • Local Image Processing: Photo resizing/cropping (e.g., 300×300 px) and signature formatting (e.g., 300×80 px) run entirely client-side via HTML5 Canvas (ImageProcessor), saving a separate processed variant without overwriting your original upload or uploading image bytes externally.
  • Storage & Cloud Exclusion: Plaintext dataUrl image payloads are omitted from IndexedDB persistence; payloads are stored encrypted with AES-256-GCM inside the documents store. Uploaded documents are strictly local-only in version 2.0.0 and are never uploaded when optional Cloud Sync is used.

6. Autofill & Active Webpage Access

JobFill BD interacts with recruitment web pages solely to help you scan form controls and insert your saved information cleanly:

  • Where Scripts Can Run: Declared content scripts target Bangladeshi government, educational, banking, and recruitment domains (*.teletalk.com.bd, *.gov.bd, *.bb.org.bd, *.org.bd, *.ac.bd, *.edu.bd, *.bdjobs.com in store packages). On other websites, the extension injects and accesses the active tab only after you explicitly interact (clicking the extension popup/action button and granting browser host permission via activeTab / optional_host_permissions when requested).
  • Strict Exclusions: Content scripts are statically excluded in manifest.json and blocked at runtime (site-policy.ts) on Google Meet, Zoom, YouTube, Google Accounts/Mail/Docs/Drive/Calendar, and internal browser pages (chrome://, edge://, store pages). JobFill BD never hooks getUserMedia, RTCPeerConnection, page fetch, or XMLHttpRequest.
  • User-Reviewed Filling & Document Transfer: Form scanning and field matching happen deterministically inside your browser (with zero calls to external AI services). Values are placed into webpage inputs and file inputs receive approved document blobs (GET_APPROVED_DOCUMENT_PAYLOAD) only after you trigger/review and approve them. If a portal serves a form over unencrypted http://, an explicit confirmation warning dialog prompts you before placing sensitive fields (such as NID, birth registration, passport, date of birth, mobile, or email).
  • No Automatic Submission or Security Bypass: JobFill BD never clicks "Final Submit", declaration checkboxes outside reviewed profile fields, or payment buttons, and never solves or bypasses CAPTCHA or SMS/OTP challenges.

7. Public Job Circular Source Requests

To keep the Job Circular Hub up to date, JobFill BD checks public recruitment notices on manual refresh or scheduled MV3 background alarms:

  • Read-Only Requests Without Profile Data: All background job circular requests use singlePublicGet() with HTTP GET and credentials: 'omit'. They do not intentionally include your saved profile fields, National ID, uploaded files, cookies, or authentication tokens.
  • Configured Public Sources (13 Adapters): Requests target public endpoints on Teletalk Alljobs (alljobs.teletalk.com.bd), Bangladesh National Portal & listed ministry/university notice pages (bangladesh.gov.bd, mopa.gov.bd, dshe.gov.bd, doict.gov.bd, dme.gov.bd, techedu.gov.bd, dghs.gov.bd, cabinet.gov.bd, mof.gov.bd, www.du.ac.bd, www.buet.ac.bd, petrobangla.org.bd), BPSC (bpsc.gov.bd), Bangladesh Bank (erecruitment.bb.org.bd), NTRCA (ntrca.gov.bd), DPE (dpe.gov.bd), Bdjobs public static GovtJobs JSON (storage.googleapis.com), Google News RSS feeds (news.google.com), Remotive (remotive.com), Remote OK (remoteok.com), Himalayas (himalayas.app), Arbeitnow (www.arbeitnow.com), and ReliefWeb API v2 (api.reliefweb.int — only if you enter your own approved free ReliefWeb appname parameter in Preferences, which is passed in the query string).
  • Anti-Bot & CAPTCHA Respect Policy: If any portal returns an anti-bot or CAPTCHA challenge page (such as Bangladesh Bank challenge gates), automated extraction stops immediately without attempting to bypass the gate, switches to manual review guidance, and enters cooldown backoff.

8. Optional Supabase Authentication & Encrypted Cloud Sync

Optional cloud features connect to a Supabase backend only when you explicitly enter a Supabase Project URL (HTTPS) and public Anon Key in Security & Backup and enable them. The extension blocks storing Supabase service_role keys inside client configuration.

A. Supabase Authentication (Opt-In Only)

  • When you sign up or sign in (supabase-auth.ts), your entered email address and password are sent over HTTPS to your configured Supabase Auth endpoints (/auth/v1/signup and /auth/v1/token?grant_type=password). Signing out notifies /auth/v1/logout when online.
  • Short-lived session access tokens are held in volatile runtime memory (never written to persistent extension storage), while your authenticatedEmail and Supabase URL/Anon Key are persisted locally in chrome.storage.local until signed out or cleared.

B. What Cloud Sync Encrypts & Transmits

When Cloud Sync is turned on and you trigger a vault upload with your local Sync Passphrase:

  • Client-Side End-to-End Encryption (E2EE) of Bundles: In encrypted-sync-service.ts, the extension serializes two JSON bundles in your browser—profiles_bundle (all local applicant profiles) and activity_bundle (job bookmarks and application tracker records)—and encrypts both client-side using Web Crypto PBKDF2-SHA256 (100,000 iterations, random 16-byte salt) + AES-256-GCM (random 12-byte IV). An envelope check (assertValidCiphertextEnvelope) blocks uploading non-encrypted data.
  • Remote Storage Scope: The resulting AES-256-GCM ciphertext payloads, schema version, client timestamp, and record_scope (profiles_bundle or activity_bundle) are uploaded to your Supabase PostgreSQL table user_vault_blobs, isolated per account via Row Level Security (auth.uid() = user_id enforced by server triggers).
  • Passphrase & Non-E2EE Metadata Boundaries: Your Sync Passphrase is never transmitted to Supabase, so Supabase servers cannot decrypt the AES-256-GCM ciphertext payloads inside profiles_bundle or activity_bundle. However, authentication inputs (email/password during login or signup), user UUID, row metadata (record_scope, schema_version, client_updated_at, updated_at), and standard network metadata (IP address) are not end-to-end encrypted and are processed by the configured Supabase host. Uploaded documents (documents_bundle) are not uploaded in version 2.0.0.

C. Optional Shared Public Job Database Feed

If you separately enable Read verified circulars from Shared Public Job Database (usePublicCloudJobsFeed), public-jobs-client.ts performs unauthenticated read-only GET requests (credentials: 'omit') to /rest/v1/jobs on the configured Supabase project to retrieve public approved job circulars. No personal profile data or user auth tokens are sent on these reads.

10. Encryption & Security Practices

  • Web Crypto AES-256-GCM at Rest: Profiles, normalized education_records, experience_records, and uploaded documents in IndexedDB are encrypted using AES-256-GCM with 12-byte random IVs. By default, keys are generated as non-extractable CryptoKey objects (extractable: false) stored natively in IndexedDB.
  • Optional Master Vault Password: Enabling a Master Vault Password derives keys client-side via 100,000 iterations of PBKDF2-SHA256 with a random 16-byte salt. Your plaintext password is never written to disk; only a PBKDF2 hash and salt are stored to verify unlocking. Unlocked keys remain only in volatile memory (KeyManager) and clear on manual lock, sign-out, or inactivity auto-lock (configurable; default 15 minutes).
  • Least-Privilege Code Hygiene: Extension pages enforce strict Content Security Policy (script-src 'self'; object-src 'self';), block eval() / new Function() / XMLHttpRequest / navigator.sendBeacon, mask sensitive numbers in local logs (PrivacyController, maskSensitiveHistoryValue), and strip query parameters/tokens from autofill history URLs.
  • Honest Scope Notes: Non-sensitive UI settings, PBKDF2 verification salt/hash metadata, adaptive CSS selector fingerprints, and optional cloud endpoint settings (URL, public anon key, email display label) are stored unencrypted in browser local storage so the extension UI can operate. In constrained non-IndexedDB test fallback environments without Web Crypto key storage, a legacy fallback path uses AES-GCM with a locally stored secret key.
Zero-Knowledge Passphrase Recovery Limitation If you enable a Master Vault Password, encrypted JSON backup passphrase, or Cloud Sync passphrase and lose both your passphrase and unencrypted backup files, neither JobFill BD nor MR Software Studio has any server-side backdoor or master key to recover your encrypted data.

11. User Consent & Cloud Sync Opt-In / Opt-Out

  • Opt-In Architecture: Cloud Sync and the Public Cloud Jobs Feed are both disabled by default (false). No remote Supabase connection is established unless you enter your endpoint credentials and enable those controls in Security & Backup.
  • Granular Action Control: Even after configuring Supabase, uploading and restoring vault data happen only when you click upload/sync or preview & pull with your sync passphrase (previewCloudVaultPull lets you inspect remote counts and choose merge or replace conflict handling before overwriting local data).
  • Opting Out Anytime: You can toggle off Cloud Sync, disable the Public Cloud Jobs Feed, click Sign Out (which clears your in-memory JWT and locks the vault), or click Delete Cloud Data at any time and continue using JobFill BD locally.

12. Data Retention & Deletion

MR Software Studio does not operate a centralized retention timer for your local device files; actual persistence follows your browser and database actions:

  • Local Device Retention: Local profiles, documents, bookmarks, and application tracker entries remain in your browser profile until you delete them or uninstall the extension. Local autofill audit history caps at the 100 most recent sessions; in-memory diagnostics hold up to 50 hostname-only events and vanish when the extension worker/tab unloads.
  • In-Extension Deletion Controls: Inside Security & Backup, Delete Local Private Vault Data (deleteLocalUserData()) clears all uploaded documents, profiles, education/experience records, vault keys, bookmarks, application tracker entries, notifications, and autofill history while preserving cached public job circulars. Purge All Profiles & Data (purgeAllData()) erases every IndexedDB store and extension storage key completely.
  • Cloud Vault Deletion Boundary: Clicking Delete Cloud Data (deleteAllPrivateCloudData()) while signed in issues authenticated HTTP DELETE calls to remove your rows from user_vault_blobs and user_sync_devices on the configured Supabase project and resets local cloud sync status. Deleting the underlying Supabase Auth login user record itself is not performed automatically by that button; it requires invoking the optional delete-account Supabase Edge Function (if deployed on your Supabase backend) or deleting the auth user directly in your Supabase project dashboard.

13. Backup & Restore

  • Manual Local Export: You can export your applicant profiles to a .json file on your computer using exportProfileData(), choosing either an AES-256-GCM password-encrypted backup (recommended) or an unencrypted JSON export. Exporting updates the local lastBackupAt metadata timestamp.
  • Restore Validation: Importing a backup file (importProfileData()) validates required profile schema structure locally and asks for the backup password if isEncrypted is true.
  • Scope Note: Profile JSON backups cover your profile, address, education, and experience fields; binary uploaded files (photos, signatures, certificates) remain inside local browser IndexedDB rather than the JSON profile export bundle. Any backup file you download to disk is protected by your device's file security and, if enabled, your chosen backup passphrase.

14. Notifications & Job Reminders

JobFill BD uses Manifest V3 browser alarms and desktop notifications to keep you informed about job circulars:

  • Local Scheduling (chrome.alarms): JobAlarmManager runs two alarms—a periodic 6-hour / daily digest alarm (jobfill_daily_job_check, default 9:00 AM Asia/Dhaka) and a single consolidated deadline alarm (jobfill_deadline_scheduler) for upcoming deadlines.
  • Local Filtering & Storage: Preference matching (preferred categories, organizations, locations, employment type, work mode, education level, minimum salary, include/exclude keywords) runs locally on your cached circulars inside the browser. Notification items are saved in IndexedDB (notifications store), and desktop alerts are rendered via chrome.notifications. Clicking a notification focuses or opens the extension Dashboard directly to that circular.

16. User Rights & Privacy Controls

You control all personal records handled by JobFill BD:

  • Access, Edit & Multi-Profile Control: Inspect, edit, switch, or delete individual applicant profiles, qualifications, employment entries, bookmarks, and uploaded documents at any time inside the Dashboard.
  • Autofill Safety & Review Mode: Choose review-first filling, require confirmation before overwriting populated fields, approve or skip individual form fields and document uploads, and dismiss the floating assistant per tab.
  • Security & Sync Autonomy: Enable/change/disable a Master Vault Password, adjust inactivity auto-lock timing, use JobFill BD entirely without an account, or opt in/out of encrypted Supabase sync independently.
  • Portability & Erasure: Export local JSON backups, clear private local vault data, purge all extension storage, delete synced cloud vault rows, or uninstall the extension from your browser.
